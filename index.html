What holds the record.

These are properties of the database, not promises in a policy.

  • Row-level securityEvery table is isolated per workspace inside Postgres. The application cannot read across a boundary even if it tries.
  • Edit log by triggerEvery create, alteration, deletion and restore is written by the database itself, with who, when and what changed. The app has no code path that writes it.
  • Soft delete onlyNothing is ever removed from the record. Deleted entries leave the reports and stay in the log.
  • Daily backupsA full database backup every Sunday and a differential every other day, shipped to Azure Blob storage, with a scripted restore drill that boots a copy and compares row counts.
  • Roles per companyFive roles, from owner to viewer, set per company inside a workspace. Invitations, not shared passwords.
  • The model reads notesA Claude model reads the contract note and nothing else. A deterministic check recomputes every figure, and you confirm before a voucher exists.

Questions a CA asks first.

Can we import from Tally?

Yes. Masters and vouchers come across through Tally XML, so opening balances and history arrive with you. The chart of accounts keeps its groups, and vouchers keep their numbers and dates.

Who owns the data?

You do. Each workspace is isolated by row-level security in Postgres. Every report exports to CSV, the GST returns export in the portal's format, and a full export of a workspace is available on request.

Do you file our returns?

No. ScripBooks generates GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B in the portal's format, Form 26Q, e-invoice and e-way bill requests. You review and file, as you do now.

Does the AI do the accounting?

No. A Claude model reads the contract note, and only the contract note. A deterministic check recomputes every figure against the note, and you confirm before a voucher exists. Every other entry is yours, on the keyboard.

Which brokers are supported?

Any broker that issues a contract note. There is no per-broker template; the reader works from the note itself, PDF or HTML, password-protected or not. The broker, trading account and demat account are matched to your masters on import.

How is profit computed?

Weighted-average cost in the books and FIFO on screen, both visible at once. Sells post at weighted-average cost so the ledger stays in the shape your auditor expects, and the FIFO column shows the gain for the tax computation.

Is it also the ledger for the operating business?

Yes. For a trading or distribution company, GST, e-invoicing, e-way bills, TDS, inventory with godowns and batches, orders, cost centres and budgets are all in the same books, in the same keyboard-first shape.
